If WhatsApp bans your number

A calm checklist for getting a restricted number reviewed and back in use.

Show the steps
  1. 1 Know which ban you have A temporary ban shows a countdown in WhatsApp. Wait it out, and don't reinstall or switch apps, because that can make it permanent.
  2. 2 Request a review Open WhatsApp on the phone, tap Request a review, and explain that these were customers who opted in.
  3. 3 If the review fails, use WhatsApp's contact form Go to whatsapp.com/contact and include the number in international format.
  4. 4 Pause here Unlink this number and stop its campaigns while the review runs. Sending from another number does not lift the ban.
  5. 5 Once it's back Link it again and answer its 2 questions again; daily sending starts low and rises as people reply. Send only to people who opted in, and vary your messages.
