Bulk sending that keeps your number safe

Numbers get blocked when they send like a machine. Wapzo sends like a careful person and holds back when something looks wrong.

  • A daily limit that grows with your number. A new number starts small; an established one sends more.
  • Random gaps and rests. No two messages leave at a machine’s even rhythm.
  • Quiet hours. Bulk sends wait overnight instead of waking your customers.
  • Automatic pause. A campaign stops when failures climb, before WhatsApp notices.
